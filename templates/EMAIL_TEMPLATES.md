## Email Cadence Strategy

### Standard Sequence:
- **Day 1:** Initial email (Template 1A, 2A, 3A, or 4A)
- **Day 4:** Follow-up #1 (Template 1B, 2B, 3B, or 4B)
- **Day 8:** Follow-up #2 (Value-add - share case study/article)
- **Day 15:** Final follow-up (Breakup email - Template 10A)

### Warm Sequence (After LinkedIn connection):
- **Day 1:** Connection request
- **Day 2:** Thank you message on LinkedIn
- **Day 4:** Email (Template 6A)
- **Day 7:** Follow-up with value
- **Day 14:** Final touch

## Subject Line Formulas

### High-Performing Patterns:
1. **Question:** "Quick question about [Company]'s [pain point]"
2. **Personalization:** "[First Name], noticed [specific observation]"
3. **Value:** "Free [service] for [Company]"
4. **Urgency:** "[Compliance requirement] deadline approaching"
5. **Social Proof:** "How we helped [Similar Company]"
6. **Direct:** "[Service] for [Company]"

### A/B Test These:
- "Quick question about [Company]'s email security"
- "[First Name], is [Company] protected from email breaches?"
- "Free email risk scan for [Company]"
- "How we saved [Similar Company] ₹50L"
- "[Company] + AKATRON = Secure future"

## Email Best Practices

### Do's:
✅ Personalize every email (name, company, specific detail)
✅ Keep it under 100 words
✅ Lead with value, not features
✅ Include clear CTA
✅ Use bullet points for readability
✅ Add social proof when possible
✅ Follow up 3-4 times
✅ Track opens and clicks
✅ Test different subject lines
✅ Send during business hours (9 AM - 5 PM IST)

### Don'ts:
❌ Generic "Dear Sir/Madam"
❌ Long paragraphs
❌ Multiple CTAs
❌ Aggressive sales language
❌ Spelling/grammar errors
❌ Sending on weekends
❌ More than 4 follow-ups
❌ Attachments in first email
❌ All caps or excessive punctuation!!!
❌ Giving up after one email

## Tracking & Optimization

### Metrics to Monitor:
- **Open Rate:** Target 25-35%
- **Reply Rate:** Target 5-10%
- **Meeting Booked:** Target 2-5%
- **Conversion to Client:** Target 20-30% of meetings

### Tools to Use:
- **Email Tracking:** Mailtrack, HubSpot, Streak
- **Email Finder:** Hunter.io, Apollo.io
- **Automation:** Lemlist, Woodpecker, Reply.io
- **CRM:** Google Sheets, HubSpot Free, Pipedrive

### Weekly Review:
- [ ] Which subject lines got highest opens?
- [ ] Which templates got most replies?
- [ ] What time/day performed best?
- [ ] Which industries responded most?
- [ ] What objections came up?
